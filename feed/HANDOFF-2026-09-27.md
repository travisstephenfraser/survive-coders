## Architecture

- `src/scenes/Songs.js`: a parallel scene loading the later songs behind the title, and
  `loopSong(scene, key, volume, onPlay, wants)`, which starts a song that lands after its scene did.
- `src/songPlayer.js`: the note-data scheduler (live on a scene clock or a timer, offline render).
  `src/elevatorSong.js`, `src/victorySong.js`: the two originals.
- Call sites: `playMusic` in Park/Tower (0.28); Chute's fall song (0.28) and the drop (0.32,
  handed to Landing); Elevator plays from `registry.elevatorSongAt`, reset in `Title.go()`.
- **Load-bearing:**
  - A scene clock is stale in `create()`: sync `this.time.now = this.game.loop.time` before
    scheduling from `now`.
  - The Chute hands its playing music to the Landing through scene data and nulls its own reference.
  - Phaser queues key events to the next step, and `sound.mute` reads back only after the next audio
    render: a check waits ~80 ms before reading either.

## Key decisions (do not relitigate)

- **Travis picked every pack song by ear**; the shortlist came from measured tempo, key and density.
- **The elevator song is original:** nothing in the pack is elevator music.
- **Loudness is matched at encode time**, so each role keeps one volume in code.
- **Later songs load behind the title:** preloading them would have added 3.7 MB before play.
- Carried forward: the landing is its own scene; the Waymo lines up under you; the window cracks
  from contracts; the waves never gate the exit; loose line matching; the photos stay local; the
  Hydra is the one boss; every level after the first is a checkpoint.
