## Architecture

- **Scenes (`src/scenes/`):** `Level1`, `Park` and `BossHQ` are PlayScenes (ASCII map → tilemap,
  with a `TILESETS` entry per theme). `HUD`, `Cine`, `Title` and `End` are overlays and screens.
- **Enemies:** `enemies.js` maps a map character to a class in `SPAWNERS`: B G H F V J.
- **Art is drawn at boot:** `sprites.js`, `backdrops.js` (`LAYERS`, `PARK_LAYERS`), `hqArt.js`
  (with its helpers now exported), `parkArt.js` (`transit_facade`, `tower_lobby`, `gondola`).
- **Load-bearing idea:** every facade uses `FACADE` (144x160, door at x = 88), so one walk-in and
  the sliding `hq_door` panels work at every building.

## Key decisions (do not relitigate)

- **Run order:** Level 1 → Park → Tower (59, 60, 61) → Chute → Waymo → Hydra, with no tower boss.
  Travis wants one continuous run, the parachute as the arrival, and the Hydra as the one boss.
- **Thin-parody names** (Agentfarce, Dreamfarce): the game is public; the landmarks keep their
  real names.
- **The enemy and mechanic picks** in the plan are Travis's; the ones he dropped stay dropped.
- **Buildings are solid, dimmer toward the back, under a sunset sky:** Travis's request. It
  overrides the 2026-09-26 "opacity ladder and haze" decision.
- **The demo grab never does damage:** a held player who didn't mash died from contact ticks
  otherwise.
- **Jets launch from the grate only:** re-entering the top of the column compounded the launch.
- **Each post-Level-1 level is a checkpoint** that heals and restores stars and tokens, for fast
  retries (design review).
